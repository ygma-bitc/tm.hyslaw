# tm.hyslaw: 'teal' Modules for Hy's Law Monitoring and Evaluation of Drug-Induced Serious Hepatotoxicity (eDISH) Plots

Provides open-source 'teal' modules and reusable builders for
exploratory monitoring of Hy's Law (Hyman Zimmerman's observation that
hepatocellular injury with jaundice predicts severe drug-induced liver
injury) from Clinical Data Interchange Standards Consortium (CDISC)
Analysis Data Model (ADaM) subject-level analysis dataset (ADSL) and
laboratory analysis dataset (ADLB) records. Peak evaluation of
Drug-Induced Serious Hepatotoxicity (eDISH), modified evaluation of
Drug-Induced Serious Hepatotoxicity (mDISH; on-treatment peaks divided
by baseline), and cholestatic screening plots are produced, and
concurrent alanine aminotransferase (ALT) or aspartate aminotransferase
(AST) plus bilirubin elevations, with optional alkaline phosphatase
(ALP) exclusion, are classified without a study-specific drug-induced
liver injury analysis dataset (ADDILI). Table and plot builders can be
called without launching 'shiny'. Hy's Law and eDISH criteria are
discussed by Temple (2006)
[doi:10.1002/pds.1211](https://doi.org/10.1002/pds.1211) , the United
States Food and Drug Administration (2009) "Drug-Induced Liver Injury:
Premarketing Clinical Evaluation"
<https://www.fda.gov/media/116737/download>, and Watkins et al. (2011)
[doi:10.2165/11586600-000000000-00000](https://doi.org/10.2165/11586600-000000000-00000)
. Baseline-scaled mDISH plots follow the fold-baseline display in Lin et
al. (2012) [doi:10.1007/BF03261982](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF03261982)
.
